# EIN Application Guide — Levee Line Research LLC

**Single-Member LLC | Disregarded Entity (default)**

Apply online at the IRS. Free. EIN issued immediately on completion.

**URL:** https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 7am–10pm ET
**Cost:** Free
**Time:** ~15 minutes, EIN issued instantly

---

## Before You Apply

You need your Articles of Organization filed and approved first. Have these ready:

- Louisiana Secretary of State **file number** (from Articles approval confirmation)
- Your **SSN** (as responsible party — **do NOT commit to git**)
- **Business formation date** (from Articles approval)

## Tax Classification — What "Disregarded Entity" Means

By default, a single-member LLC is a **disregarded entity** for federal tax purposes:

- The LLC itself files **no separate federal income tax return**.
- All Company income, deductions, and credits flow directly onto your personal **Form 1040, Schedule C** (Profit or Loss from Business).
- Self-employment tax (15.3% — 12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare, on first $168,600 for 2026) applies to net Schedule C earnings.
- No K-1s. No partnership return. No capital-account math.
- For state purposes, Louisiana follows the federal classification.

### What about S-Corp election?

You CAN elect S-corp taxation by filing **Form 2553** with the IRS. Pros and cons:

**Pros:**
- Can reduce self-employment tax once net income is high enough
- You pay yourself a "reasonable salary" via W-2 (subject to FICA), and the rest comes through as a distribution (not subject to self-employment tax)

**Cons:**
- Requires running payroll (additional administrative cost — Gusto, ADP, etc.)
- Requires filing **Form 1120-S** annually
- "Reasonable salary" rule means you can't pay yourself $1 — IRS expects industry-appropriate compensation
- Generally not worth it until net income exceeds ~$50,000/year

**Recommendation:** Stay as disregarded entity now. Revisit S-corp election once a grant lands and revenue makes the math work. Form 2553 deadline is 2.5 months into the tax year you want it to start (e.g., March 15, 2027 to start S-corp for 2027 tax year), so there's no rush.

### Grant implications

- Disregarded-entity status is fully acceptable for all federal grant programs (SBIR/STTR, USDA NIFA, NSF, DOE, NASA, NRCS).
- SBIR/STTR programs do not restrict based on tax classification.
- Keep grant funds segregated in accounting (use a dedicated class/project code) — grant auditors check fund flow.

## After You Receive Your EIN

1. **Save the EIN confirmation letter (CP 575)** immediately. You only get one — losing it is a 6-week headache to reissue. Print, PDF, and back it up.

2. **Required for next steps:**
   - Business bank account
   - SAM.gov registration
   - Grants.gov registration
   - Louisiana Department of Revenue registration

3. **Treat the EIN like sensitive information** — don't post it publicly, don't commit it to git.
