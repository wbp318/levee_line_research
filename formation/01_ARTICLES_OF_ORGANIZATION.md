## After Filing

1. You receive a confirmation with your **File Number** — save this immediately.
2. Online filings are typically processed same-day to within a few business days.
3. Once approved, order a **certified copy** ($30) — required for bank account and grant registrations.
4. Move directly to the EIN application (Document 03).

## Why Single-Member, Not Multi-Member

The structure pivoted to single-member on 2026-05-11. William Brooks Parker is the sole operator. Three reasons this is the right call:

1. **Simpler taxes.** Single-member LLC is a **disregarded entity** for federal tax purposes by default — income flows directly onto your personal Schedule C. No Form 1065 partnership return, no K-1s, no capital accounts. Saves $500–$1,500/year in accountant fees.

2. **No SBIR affiliation contamination.** Adding members who own/control other businesses (which Nolan and Matthew do) would pull those companies' employees and revenue into Levee Line's SBIR size-standard math under 13 CFR 121.103. That risk is eliminated as a single-member LLC.

3. **Faster decisions, no governance overhead.** No supermajority votes, no operating agreement amendments to add members, no buy-out terms to negotiate when relationships change.

Nolan and Matthew can still contribute as **advisors** (no equity, listed in management-team narrative on grant applications) or, if they want to put capital in, as **investors via convertible debt** rather than direct membership. See the Operating Agreement, Article XV, for the advisor framework.
