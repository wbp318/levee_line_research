## What You Need to SHOW

### For SBIR / STTR / AFRI grants

1. **One-paragraph problem statement** — what specific problem you solve
2. **PI biosketch** (5-page NIH format) — your background, education, prior work
3. **Specific Aims page** — 3–5 measurable, time-bounded objectives
4. **Preliminary evidence** — a notebook, a small prototype, a labeled dataset. Doesn't have to be polished
5. **3–5 end-user letters of support** — growers, co-ops, extension agents
6. **One university letter of support** — LSU AgCenter or UADA
7. **Data Management Plan** (template in `research/09_AG_RESEARCH_SCOPE.md`)
8. **Commercialization Plan** — who buys this, at what price, market size, Phase III path
9. **Budget + justification** — line items, with reasoning
10. **Active SAM.gov registration + UEI**

### For SBA loans / bank credit

1. Personal credit score (pull and clean up)
2. EIN letter
3. Articles of Organization
4. Operating Agreement
5. 12-month cash flow projection
6. Personal guarantee (banks will require this regardless of LLC structure)

### For Louisiana state programs

1. LA-domiciled LLC + active LA Secretary of State status
2. QRE tracking log (for R&D Tax Credit)
3. Federal SBIR/STTR award notice (for IRG — reactive only)
