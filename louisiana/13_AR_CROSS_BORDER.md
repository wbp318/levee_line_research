# AR Cross-Border R&D Access (LA-Domiciled Path)

_Written 2026-05-11 to address: "Can a Louisiana-domiciled LLC access Arkansas funds if some of the research work happens in Arkansas?"_

**Short answer:** Yes, partially — and the highest-ROI move is signing a research subcontract with **UAPB** or **UADA Rohwer Research Station**. That single move unlocks Arkansas's **University-Based R&D Tax Credit (33%, no statewide cap published)** without requiring any address change, foreign-LLC registration, or relocation. Programs that require AR domicile or AR employees (Advantage Arkansas, Targeted Business Payroll Credit, most ADFA SSBCI programs) stay closed unless Levee Line opens an actual AR business location.

Lake Providence, LA is **15 mi** from the AR state line. Pine Bluff (UAPB) is ~100 mi. Rohwer Research Station (Desha County) is ~30 mi. Drivable for field-day work, monthly project meetings, in-person data collection.

## Program-by-Program Verdict

| AR Program | Accessible Without AR Domicile? | What It Takes | LA-LLC Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AEDC University-Based R&D Tax Credit (33%, no cap stated)** | **Yes** | Signed research contract with an AR university (UAPB, UADA, UAM, UA-Fayetteville, UALR, UAMS, A-State). QREs paid under that contract qualify. | **Highest ROI** — pursue this first. |
| **ARA Impact Grants (industry-partner role)** | **Yes** | PI must be at AR partner institution. Levee Line provides industry match. $75K cap, 20% match. | Strong path — watch for ARA 4.0 RFP cycle. |
| **UAPB 1890 Capacity Building Grants (subaward)** | **Yes** | UAPB primes; LLC subawarded for work that "advances capacity building at the 1890 institution." $100K–$750K awards, $28M annual pool. | Strong — requires UAPB faculty PI sponsor. |
| **AEDC SBIR Matching Grant** (Phase I up to $50K; Phase II up to $100K) | **Maybe** — spec uses "principally involved" language; AR domicile not explicitly required, but practical pattern is AR-domiciled awardees | Federal SBIR award + ties to AR-targeted sectors | Verify with AEDC Sci/Tech Division before counting on it from LA. |
| **AEDC Targeted Business R&D Credit (33%, $50K/yr × 5)** | Partial | All QREs must be **incurred in Arkansas**. Out-of-state QREs do not qualify. | Possible only if some work is genuinely performed at AR field sites or AR contractor offices. |
| **Equity Investment Tax Credit (33⅓%, transferable once)** | Partial | Investors must be AR-resident or AR-business. Foreign-registered AR LLC may qualify if structured as AR investment vehicle. | Niche — only worth it if specific AR investors want the credit. |
| **Advantage Arkansas (4% payroll, Tier 4 in Chicot/Desha/Ashley)** | **No** | Requires AR business location + AR-resident employees at min wage thresholds. | Closed to LA-domiciled LLC. |
| **Targeted Business Payroll Credit (10% × 5)** | **No** | Same — requires AR-located business. | Closed. |
| **ADFA SSBCI direct programs** (loan participation, guarantee, VC) | **No (most likely)** | Generally requires AR operating company. Treasury SSBCI lets states define; AR's pattern is operating-presence. | Closed; LA's own $113M SSBCI is closer and friendlier. |
| **NSF EPSCoR DART (AR data-science center)** | **Indirect only** | DART is an institutional research center, not a company grant. | Could be industry partner or hire DART graduates. Not a direct funding source. |

## Highest-ROI Action Sequence (Without Relocating)

### Step 1: Pick the AR university counterparty

Two strong options based on geography and research fit:

**UAPB (Pine Bluff, ~100 mi)**
- 1890 land-grant — federal Evans-Allen capacity formula + CBG ($100K–$750K) pipeline only available at 1890 institutions
- Strong AI-for-aquaculture and ag-AI work already running
- AQFI Director Dr. Yathish Ramena
- ORSP for sponsored programs: orsp@uapb.edu / (870) 575-8051
- Best for: AI-ag projects with rural/historically-underserved farmer framing

**UADA Rohwer Research Station (Desha County, ~30 mi)**
- 634-acre Delta row-crop research hub — 175 field trials/year, 18,000 plots/year
- Closest physical research asset, drivable for daily field work
- Resident Director Linda Martin — (870) 673-6100
- UADA Technology Commercialization Office: agritco@uada.edu / (479) 575-2608
- Best for: row-crop yield/disease/precision-ag work with on-the-ground field validation

For Levee Line's flagship A1541 DSFAS concept (Delta row-crop yield-gap prediction), **UADA Rohwer is the closer and tighter fit on physical assets**. UAPB is the right choice if the broader-impacts framing wants 1890 land-grant equity emphasis.

You can do both — they are not exclusive.

### Step 2: Negotiate a research subcontract

Structure as a fee-for-research contract with the AR institution. Levee Line pays the AR institution to perform some scope of work (e.g., field validation, ground-truth labeling, agronomic analysis), and the contract value plus Levee Line's own AR-incurred QREs under the project go into the University-Based R&D Tax Credit basis.

Typical structure:
- Statement of work: 6–18 months
- Budget: $25K–$75K to start, scaled to project size
- Deliverables: dataset, technical report, possibly co-authored publication
- IP: pre-existing IP retained by each party; jointly developed IP terms negotiated upfront

Key gotcha: the 33% credit applies to QREs **performed in Arkansas**. Levee Line's own out-of-state QREs (Lake Providence office, LA-based cloud costs) do NOT count toward the AR credit — they go toward the LA R&D Tax Credit instead. Set up accounting to track LA-incurred vs AR-incurred separately.

### Step 3: Optional — foreign-LLC registration in AR

**Cost:** ~$270 online filing + $155/yr franchise tax + AR registered agent (~$49–$125/yr).

**Worth it only if:**
- You want to access EITC (33⅓% Equity Investment Tax Credit) and have AR-taxpayer investors interested
- You're stacking enough AR programs that the administrative overhead pays off

**Not worth it for the University-Based R&D Credit alone** — that credit doesn't require foreign-LLC registration, just the AR-university research contract.

### Step 4 (much later, if ever): Open an AR business location

Only justifies the overhead if Levee Line wants Advantage Arkansas (4% payroll) and Targeted Business Payroll Credit (10% × 5). Requires:
- Physical AR business address
- At least one AR-resident employee on payroll at min wage thresholds
- Operating presence on the books

This is a Year 3+ decision once headcount and revenue justify it.

## What This Looks Like in Practice

**Realistic Year-1 cross-border posture:**
1. LA-domiciled LLC, all main operations in Lake Providence
2. Signed research contract with UAPB **or** UADA Rohwer for ~$50K of joint work over 12 months
3. ~$50K of QREs counted against AR University-Based R&D Credit → ~$16,500 credit
4. Remaining ~$75K+ of QREs counted against LA R&D Tax Credit → up to ~$22,500 (at 30% rate)
5. No foreign-LLC registration in AR
6. No AR employees
7. Letter of support from UAPB / UADA partner on USDA SBIR and AFRI A1541 applications

This delivers the credit stack and the university bench without any of the relocation friction.

## What's Off the Table (And Why)

| Program | Why It's Closed |
|---|---|
| Advantage Arkansas (4% payroll, Tier 4) | Requires AR business location + AR-resident employees |
| Targeted Business Payroll Credit (10% × 5) | Same requirement |
| ADFA SSBCI direct programs | Generally requires AR operating company |
| AR Venture Capital Development Fund (ADFA, up to $1M direct equity) | Requires AR operating company |
| NSF EPSCoR DART direct funding | DART is an institutional research center, not a company grant program |

LA's own programs cover the same gaps without the relocation tax: Innovation Retention Grant ($100K), LA SSBCI ($113M pool), LA Growth Fund (equity), LA R&D Tax Credit (5–30%). The AR programs above were attractive only as part of the relocation plan that's now shelved.
